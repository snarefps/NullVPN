## ✨ Features

- **One tap to connect** — with live speed, session time and ping.
- **Subscriptions** — add by link, QR code or clipboard; update them in one tap.
- **Ping all, pick the fastest** — test every server and connect to the best one.
- **Smart routing** — send local sites direct, block what you don't want, proxy the rest.
- **DNS control** — DoH, DoT, DoQ and plain resolvers, with FakeDNS.
- **Aether (WARP)** — built-in Cloudflare WARP and MASQUE, with optional Tor.
- **Two ways to tunnel on Windows** — full TUN mode or system proxy.
- **Per-app routing on Android** — choose which apps use the VPN.
- **Quick Settings toggle on Android** — connect from the notification panel, even with the app closed.
- **English and فارسی** — full right-to-left support with the Estedad font.
- **Light and dark themes.**

## 🚀 Quick start

1. Install NullVPN from the table above.
2. Open **Servers** and add your servers — paste a subscription link, scan a QR code, or import from the clipboard.
3. Tap **Ping all** to find the fastest server.
4. Go back to **Home** and tap the big button to connect.

## 🔒 Privacy

NullVPN has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It only talks to:

- the servers and subscription links **you** add;
- `ip-api.com`, to show your current IP address and country;
- a public relay list, only when you choose an exit location in Aether.

Sending a device ID to subscription providers is **off by default**, and you can turn it on in Settings if your provider limits devices.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Which Android file should I download?</b></summary>
<br>
Almost every phone from the last several years needs <b>arm64-v8a</b>. Use <b>armeabi-v7a</b> only on old 32-bit phones, and <b>x86_64</b> only for emulators.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?</b></summary>
<br>
The warning appears because the installer is not code-signed yet, not because anything is wrong with it. Click <b>More info → Run anyway</b>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Google Play Protect warns me when installing on Android. Is it safe?</b></summary>
<br>
Play Protect warns about apps installed from outside Google Play that it hasn't scanned yet. Tap <b>Scan</b> to have it checked, or tap <b>More info → Install</b>. Both are fine.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does the installer ask for administrator access?</b></summary>
<br>
TUN mode creates a virtual network adapter, and Windows only allows that with administrator rights.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does NullVPN come with servers?</b></summary>
<br>
NullVPN is a client: you add your own servers or subscriptions. The built-in Aether option connects through Cloudflare WARP without any server of your own.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I add the Quick Settings toggle on Android?</b></summary>
<br>
Pull down the notification panel, tap <b>Edit</b>, and drag <b>NullVPN</b> into your toggles.
</details>
